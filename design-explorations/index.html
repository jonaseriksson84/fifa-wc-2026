WC26 · design exploration

Three flavours.

Three completely different aesthetic directions for the same picks page, leaderboard, and pool branding. Same data; same information architecture; totally different feeling. Click to view, then send back the one you want us to actually build (or pieces from each).

01

Panini sticker album

Vintage sports nostalgia. Cream paper grain, hand-rotated cards, ink stamps, condensed sports headlines. Feels like flipping through a tournament wall chart.

Open
01b

Panini, restrained

Same soul as 01 but quieter. No noise overlay, no card rotations, two accent colours instead of four, the ink-stamp result kept as the single signature moment. More editorial-magazine.

Open
01c

Panini, in-between

The middle ground. v1b's discipline plus a faint paper grain, soft drop-shadows so cards float, a red ribbon at the top of every card, a bigger two-line ink stamp (FULL TIME · 1–0), and tiny ★★★ ornaments between stages.

Open
01d

Panini, the album fills up

v1's full maximalism plus the metaphor doing real work: future fixtures are dashed empty slots with "?" placeholders, past fixtures are stamped/stuck stickers. Knockouts foil-up by tier — pearl → holo → gold → ★ Legendary ★ Final with an animated rainbow shine. Album progress bar at the top.

Open
02

Stadium scoreboard

Pitch-black arcade. CRT scanlines, dot-matrix amber digits, neon cyan, "PRESS START" energy. Picks feel like punching a vending-machine button.

Open
03

Carnival / tropicalia

Hot-pink street parade. Caprasimo display + handwritten ribbons, falling confetti, oversized buttons that bounce on click. Joyful, loud, samba-coded.
